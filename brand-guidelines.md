# EarnPlayApps (EPA) — Brand Guidelines

## Brand positioning
EarnPlayApps is an independent software studio building useful, innovative and user-friendly mobile applications and digital solutions for users worldwide.

**Brand character:** refined, capable, modern, trustworthy, ambitious and approachable.

**Brand promise:** Building Useful Digital Experiences.

## Core visual identity

### Official palette
| Name | Hex | Role |
|---|---|---|
| Champagne Gold | `#C58B45` | Primary brand accent, logo finish, key highlights |
| Obsidian Black | `#17191D` | Premium dark surfaces, headings and high-contrast layouts |
| Ivory Pearl | `#F3EFE7` | Primary light background and calm content surfaces |
| Deep Emerald | `#263B38` | Secondary accent for depth and selective emphasis |

### Colour balance
- Ivory Pearl and clean neutral space should carry most everyday website and product interfaces.
- Obsidian Black anchors premium sections, navigation and high-contrast elements.
- Champagne Gold is a deliberate accent, not a fill colour for every control.
- Deep Emerald is used sparingly for secondary highlights and supporting details.
- Maintain accessible text contrast. Never place small gold text on a light ivory background.

## Logo direction
- Primary mark: a custom geometric lettermark combining **E**, **P** and **A** into one recognisable symbol.
- The mark must remain legible at app-icon size and work in one colour.
- Preferred finish: restrained champagne-gold metallic treatment; also provide flat single-colour versions for reliable reproduction.
- Wordmark: **EarnPlayApps**, set in a clean, contemporary sans-serif with carefully balanced spacing.
- Required lockups: gold mark on Obsidian Black; Obsidian mark on Ivory Pearl; monochrome mark for small or constrained uses.
- Avoid generic circuit icons, game controllers, coin imagery, excessive glow, bevels and noisy gradients.

## Typography
- Headings: a modern geometric or humanist sans-serif with confident weight.
- Body: a highly legible neutral sans-serif.
- Use clear hierarchy, generous but efficient spacing, and short readable line lengths.
- Avoid decorative display fonts for interface text.

## Digital product style
- Mobile-first, responsive and accessible.
- Premium, bright and uncluttered by default; use dark sections selectively.
- Subtle glass effects may be used for supporting surfaces, never at the expense of readability.
- Use consistent radii, spacing, icon stroke weights and button hierarchy.
- Prefer crisp vector icons over emoji in official product interfaces.

## Voice and messaging
- Clear, helpful and confident.
- Explain benefits in plain language; avoid exaggerated claims.
- Use international English as the website default, with accurate localisation for supported languages.

## Brand consistency
Apply this system across the EarnPlayApps website, app icons, splash screens, product interfaces, release graphics, support materials and future digital products. Individual apps may have their own product accent colours, but the EarnPlayApps parent identity should remain recognisable.
