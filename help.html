01 / INSTALLATION

Installation & downloads

Important information before installing an Android app.

How do I install an APK from EarnPlayApps?

When an app is officially released, open its product page and use the verified download link. After downloading, open the APK file and follow Android's installation prompts. Android may ask you to allow installation from the browser or file manager you used. Only install APK files obtained from the official EarnPlayApps website.

Why can't I download an app yet?

Our current applications are still in development. Download links will be enabled only after the app has completed testing and an official release is ready. We do not provide unofficial or unverified APK downloads.

What should I do if Android blocks an APK installation?

Check that your device supports the app and that the APK came from our official website. Review Android's installation permission for the app you used to open the file. Do not bypass a security warning if the file source or integrity is uncertain.

02 / PRODUCT GUIDES

Using EarnPlayApps products

General guidance for our current app projects.

What is All Convert?

All Convert is being developed as a mobile file-conversion workspace. The planned experience includes selecting a file, choosing an output format, reviewing the result and saving or sharing it. Supported formats and final features will be confirmed in the official release notes.

What can QR Scanner scan?

QR Scanner is being developed for QR codes and barcodes. Planned features include scanning with the camera, selecting an image from the gallery and reviewing scan results. Final supported barcode formats will be listed when the app is released.

What is E-Calculator?

E-Calculator is an everyday calculator project with a clear mobile keypad and a small collection of practical calculation tools. Its final tool list will be published with the official release.

03 / PLANS & PRICING

Free and Pro plans

Understand the proposed plans before sales become available.

Are the Pro plans available to purchase now?

No. Any prices currently displayed are proposed launch prices for planning purposes only. Checkout is not active and this website does not collect payments for these plans.

What is the difference between Monthly, Yearly and Lifetime?

These are proposed access options. The exact features, renewal terms, duration, device limits and Lifetime access conditions will be clearly published before any plan is offered for sale.

Will I be charged if I view a Pro plan?

No. Viewing a plan does not create a purchase or subscription. No payment is taken through the website at this time.

04 / TROUBLESHOOTING

Common issues

Try these basic checks before contacting support.

An app closes or does not open. What should I do?

Restart your device, check for available app updates and make sure your Android version is supported. If the issue continues, contact support with the app name, version, device model and the steps that caused the problem.

How do I report a bug or suggest a feature?

Email earnplayapps@gmail.com with the product name, app version, device model and a clear description. For a feature suggestion, explain what you would like the app to do and how it would help.

Where can I find the latest release information?

Visit our Updates page for official announcements, version details and release notes. We will publish information there when verified updates are available.
